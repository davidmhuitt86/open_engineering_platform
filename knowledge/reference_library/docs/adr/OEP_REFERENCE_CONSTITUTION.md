# Preamble

The Engineering Reference Library (ERL) exists to preserve, organize, execute, and distribute verified engineering knowledge.

It is the authoritative foundation upon which the Open Engineering Platform builds engineering intelligence.

The Engineering Reference Library is not documentation.

It is structured engineering knowledge.

Its purpose is to provide deterministic, reusable engineering facts that remain independent of software implementation, user interface, artificial intelligence, and computing platform.

All engineering knowledge within the Engineering Reference Library shall conform to this Constitution.

---

# Article I

## Engineering Knowledge is Authoritative

Engineering Knowledge Objects are the authoritative representation of engineering facts.

Applications shall consume Engineering Knowledge.

Applications shall never redefine Engineering Knowledge.

---

# Article II

## Engineering Knowledge is Universal

Engineering knowledge shall remain independent of:

- programming language
- operating system
- repository implementation
- database technology
- user interface
- AI provider

Knowledge shall outlive software.

---

# Article III

## Everything is an Engineering Knowledge Object

Every engineering concept shall exist as an Engineering Knowledge Object.

Examples include:

- components
- equations
- units
- symbols
- standards
- theories
- materials
- measurement methods
- engineering processes
- behaviors
- validation rules

No engineering concept shall exist outside the Engineering Knowledge Object model.

---

# Article IV

## Relationships are First-Class Engineering Citizens

Relationships are engineering knowledge.

Relationships possess:

- identity
- provenance
- version
- engineering meaning

Relationships shall never be treated as anonymous database links.

---

# Article V

## Engineering Knowledge Shall Never Be Duplicated

Engineering facts shall exist once.

Knowledge shall be referenced.

Never copied.

If two Engineering Knowledge Objects contain identical engineering facts, the architecture shall be reconsidered.

---

# Article VI

## Engineering Behavior Belongs to Knowledge

Engineering calculations belong to Engineering Behaviors.

Applications invoke Engineering Behaviors.

Applications never duplicate engineering calculations.

---

# Article VII

## Runtime Consumes Compiled Knowledge

Authoring files exist for engineers.

Compiled packages exist for software.

Runtime systems shall never consume authoring files directly.

---

# Article VIII

## Engineering Must Remain Offline

The Engineering Reference Library shall remain fully functional without Internet access.

Cloud services enhance engineering.

They shall never become mandatory.

---

# Article IX

## Artificial Intelligence Shall Consume Engineering Knowledge

Artificial Intelligence is a consumer of Engineering Knowledge.

Artificial Intelligence is never the authoritative source of engineering truth.

Engineering Knowledge Objects remain authoritative.

---

# Article X

## Deterministic Engineering

Engineering calculations shall remain deterministic.

The same Engineering Knowledge Object shall always produce the same engineering result when given identical inputs.

---

# Article XI

## Provenance is Permanent

Every Engineering Knowledge Object shall record:

- origin
- authorship
- review history
- verification
- revision history

Engineering history shall never be destroyed.

---

# Article XII

## Engineering Knowledge is Governed

Engineering Knowledge shall progress through a defined lifecycle.

No Engineering Knowledge Object becomes authoritative without review.

No Engineering Knowledge Object shall be silently modified.

---

# Article XIII

## Extensibility Without Modification

The Engineering Reference Library shall support unlimited expansion.

Future engineering disciplines shall extend the library.

They shall not require redesign of existing Engineering Knowledge Objects.

---

# Article XIV

## Marketplace Extends the Library

Marketplace packages extend engineering knowledge.

They shall never replace the Core Engineering Reference Library.

Core Engineering Knowledge remains vendor-neutral.

---

# Article XV

## Search is Discovery

Engineering Discovery is more than text search.

Discovery shall utilize:

- Engineering Knowledge Objects
- Relationships
- Behaviors
- Engineering Context
- Project Context

Discovery assists engineering understanding.

---

# Article XVI

## Engineering Knowledge is Executable

Engineering knowledge is not passive documentation.

Engineering Knowledge Objects may expose executable Engineering Behaviors.

Knowledge therefore becomes:

- calculable
- verifiable
- teachable
- explainable
- simulatable

---

# Article XVII

## Every Engineering Fact Shall Be Traceable

Every engineering conclusion shall be traceable to:

- Engineering Knowledge Objects
- Relationships
- Behaviors
- Validation
- Simulation
- Provenance

Users shall always be able to inspect the engineering basis of a result.

---

# Article XVIII

## Stability Before Convenience

Architectural stability shall take precedence over implementation convenience.

Temporary implementation shortcuts shall never become permanent architecture.

---

# Article XIX

## Long-Term Stewardship

The Engineering Reference Library is intended to outlive individual software versions, programming languages, database engines, operating systems, and artificial intelligence providers.

Engineering knowledge is preserved for future generations of engineers.

---

# Article XX

## Constitutional Authority

This Constitution governs every implementation of the Engineering Reference Library.

All future specifications, source code, packages, and engineering knowledge shall conform to this Constitution.

Conflicts between implementation and this Constitution shall be resolved in favor of this Constitution.

---

# Ratification

This Constitution establishes the permanent governing principles of the Engineering Reference Library for the Open Engineering Platform.

These principles shall remain stable unless superseded through formal constitutional revision.
