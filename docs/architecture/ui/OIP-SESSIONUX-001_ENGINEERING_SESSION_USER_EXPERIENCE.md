# 1. Purpose

This document defines how engineers interact with Engineering Sessions throughout the OEP Instruments platform.

The objective is to make Sessions feel like working at a professional engineering workstation rather than connecting a mobile application.

Every instrument shall participate in a common Session experience.

---

# 2. Philosophy

The engineer does not connect a phone.

The engineer begins an Engineering Session.

The Session becomes the engineering workspace.

Instruments may come and go.

The Session remains.

---

Consistency

Continuity

Professional workflow

# 23. Core Principles

1.

The Session is the engineer's workspace.

2.

Instruments participate in Sessions.

3.

Sessions persist independently of devices.

4.

Engineering context is never lost.

5.

History remains permanent.

6.

Collaboration is built upon Sessions.

7.

Recovery shall be automatic whenever possible.

8.

Every engineering activity belongs to a Session.
