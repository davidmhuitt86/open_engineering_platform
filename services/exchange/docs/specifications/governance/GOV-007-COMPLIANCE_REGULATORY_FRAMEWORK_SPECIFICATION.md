# GOV-007
# Compliance & Regulatory Framework Specification

**Specification ID:** GOV-007

**Title:** Compliance & Regulatory Framework Specification

**Status:** Draft 1.0

**Authority:** Open Engineering Platform Specification

**Depends On:**

- GOV-001 through GOV-006
- Repository Architecture
- Engineering Object Model

---

# 1. Purpose

This specification defines how compliance obligations, regulatory requirements, industry standards, organizational policies, and contractual obligations are represented, managed, verified, and audited within the Open Engineering Platform.

Compliance is treated as governed engineering knowledge rather than external documentation.

---

# 2. Design Goals

The Compliance Framework shall be:

- Standards-neutral
- Evidence-based
- Traceable
- Auditable
- Version-aware
- Extensible
- Repository-native
- Automation-friendly

---

# 3. Philosophy

Compliance is demonstrated through engineering evidence.

It is not established by declarations alone.

Every compliance claim shall be traceable to supporting engineering artifacts.

---

# 4. Scope

Compliance may apply to:

Engineering Requirements

Packages

Repositories

Engineering Objects

Engineering Decisions

Validation Procedures

Test Results

Engineering Calculations

Organizations

Projects

Processes

Manufactured Products

Software Systems

Organizations may extend compliance to additional artifact types.

---

# 5. Compliance Authorities

Compliance obligations may originate from:

Government Regulations

Industry Standards

International Standards

Military Specifications

OEM Requirements

Customer Contracts

Academic Policies

Internal Engineering Standards

Organizational Policies

Every compliance source shall possess a unique identifier.

# 6. Compliance Objects

The platform recognizes Compliance Objects including:

Compliance Requirement

Compliance Standard

Compliance Control

Compliance Assessment

Compliance Finding

Compliance Exception

Compliance Evidence

Compliance Certification

Compliance Objects are first-class Engineering Objects.

Laboratory Results

Simulation Outputs

Engineering Decision Records

Organizations may conduct assessments including:

External Audit

Self Assessment

Supplier Assessment

Customer Assessment

Regulatory Assessment

---

# 10. Compliance Findings

Findings may include:

Compliant

Non-Compliant

Observation

Recommendation

Opportunity for Improvement

---

# 11. Exceptions

Organizations may authorize Compliance Exceptions.

Every exception shall include:

Unique Exception ID

Authority

Justification

Scope

Risk Assessment

Mitigation Plan

Expiration Date

Approval Signatures

Exceptions are governed artifacts.

---

# 12. Certifications

Organizations may record certifications including:

ISO Certifications

Professional Certifications

OEM Certifications

Government Certifications

Laboratory Certifications

Product Certifications

Certification history shall be preserved.

---

# 13. Continuous Compliance

The platform may continuously evaluate:

Traceability

Evidence Completeness

Policy Compliance

Missing Documentation

Expired Certifications

Outstanding Findings

Broken Relationships

Continuous evaluation is policy-driven.

---

# 14. Compliance Reporting

Reports may include:

Compliance Status

Coverage

Outstanding Findings

Exceptions

Certification Status

Evidence Completeness

Audit History

Reports are generated from repository data.

---

# 15. Change Impact

Changes affecting compliance shall identify:

Affected Standards

Affected Requirements

Affected Certifications

Affected Evidence

Affected Decisions

Required Reassessment

Impact analysis is relationship-driven.

---

# 16. Governance

Compliance activities shall follow Governance Workflows.

Assessments, certifications, findings, and exceptions require appropriate approvals according to organizational policy.

---

# 17. Events

Examples include:

Compliance Requirement Created

Assessment Started

Assessment Completed

Finding Issued

Finding Closed

Exception Approved

Certification Granted

Certification Expired

Compliance Report Generated

Events become immutable governance records.

---

# 18. Audit

Audit records preserve:

Assessment History

Finding History

Exception History

Certification History

Evidence History

Approvals

Relationships

No compliance record shall be silently modified.

---

# 19. Future Extensions

Future specifications may introduce:

Automated standards interpretation

AI-assisted compliance analysis

Cross-organization compliance reporting

Regulatory synchronization

Continuous supplier compliance

Digital certification exchange

without modifying the Compliance Framework.

---

# 20. Conformance

An implementation claiming compliance with GOV-007 shall:

- Support Compliance Objects as first-class engineering artifacts.
- Preserve compliance history.
- Support evidence-based compliance.
- Support compliance relationships.
- Support continuous compliance evaluation.
- Support compliance reporting.
- Maintain immutable compliance audit records.
