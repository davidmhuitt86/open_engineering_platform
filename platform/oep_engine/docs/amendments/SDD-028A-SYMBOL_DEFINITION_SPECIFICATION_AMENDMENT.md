# SDD-028A

# Symbol Definition Specification Amendment

Status: Proposed

Version: 1.1

Amends:
SDD-028 Symbol Definition Specification

---

# Purpose

Expand Symbol Definitions into complete engineering assets.

---

# Amendment 1

## Symbol Metadata

Symbols may include:

Manufacturer

Part Number

Standard

Revision

Category

Aliases

Description

Keywords

Tags

---

# Amendment 2

## Rule Associations

Symbols may reference:

Validation Rules

Drafting Rules

Simulation Rules

AI Guidance

Training Content

These references are declarative.

---

# Amendment 3

## Marketplace Identity

Every Symbol Definition shall expose:

Package Identifier

Publisher

Version

License

Signature

Dependency Information

---

# Amendment 4

## Symbol Behaviors

Future symbol behavior shall be provided by extensions.

Examples:

Simulation

Animation

Diagnostics

Visualization

Behaviors are optional.

Symbols remain data-driven.

---

# Amendment 5

## Rendering Independence

Symbol Definitions shall remain independent of rendering technology.

Supported renderers may include:

Flutter

SVG

PDF

DXF

PNG

Print

Future renderers

The Symbol Definition shall never contain renderer-specific logic.

---

# Amendment 6

## Engineering Knowledge

Symbols may reference:

Knowledge Objects

Specifications

Procedures

Training

Evidence

Engineering Graph Nodes

These references enrich engineering context.

They do not change symbol identity.

---

# Architectural Principle

A Symbol is an engineering asset.

Rendering is one consumer of that asset.
