# EXC-010
# Commerce & Revenue Distribution Specification

**Specification ID:** EXC-010

**Title:** Commerce & Revenue Distribution Specification

**Status:** Draft 1.0

**Authority:** Open Engineering Platform Specification

**Depends On:**

- EXC-001 through EXC-009
- PKG-001 through PKG-008

---

# 1. Purpose

This specification defines how engineering packages, subscriptions, services, and other digital engineering assets are commercially exchanged through the Open Engineering Exchange.

The Commerce System manages financial transactions.

It does not define licensing rights, package ownership, or engineering content.

Those responsibilities remain defined by other specifications.

---

# 2. Design Goals

The Commerce System shall be:

- Publisher focused
- Transparent
- Auditable
- Secure
- Extensible
- International
- Tax aware
- Enterprise ready

---

# 3. Commerce Philosophy

The Engineering Exchange exists to create a sustainable engineering ecosystem.

Commerce shall support both commercial and non-commercial engineering knowledge.

Publishers determine whether engineering assets are:

Free

Paid

Donation Supported

Subscription Based

Institution Licensed

OEM Bundled

Government Contracted

Commerce shall never require every package to be commercial.

---

# 4. Commercial Assets

Commercial assets may include:

Engineering Packages

Package Collections

Engineering Libraries

Reference Data

Training Courses

Certification Programs

Support Plans

Engineering Services

Consulting Deliverables

Digital Engineering Tools

Future asset types may be introduced without changing the commerce architecture.

---

# 5. Pricing Models

Supported pricing models include:

Free

Fixed Price

Subscription

Usage Based

Tiered Pricing

Educational Pricing

Enterprise Pricing

Volume Pricing

Promotional Pricing

Custom Contracts

Pricing models are defined by the Publisher.

---

# 6. Revenue Distribution

The Exchange distributes revenue according to publisher agreements.

Distribution may include:

Publisher Share

Exchange Service Fee

Affiliate Revenue

Partner Revenue

Tax Collection

Payment Processing Fees

Revenue calculations shall be transparent and auditable.

---

# 7. Revenue Transparency

Publishers shall have access to reports including:

Gross Revenue

Exchange Fees

Taxes

Refunds

Net Revenue

Subscriptions

Regional Sales

Revenue history shall be retained.

---

# 8. Purchases

Commerce supports acquisition through:

Direct Purchase

Subscription

Institution Assignment

Enterprise Assignment

OEM Distribution

Academic Distribution

Voucher Redemption

Promotional Codes

Approved Purchase Orders

Purchases result in entitlements as defined in EXC-005.

---

# 9. Refunds

Publishers may define refund policies subject to Exchange policy.

Refund events include:

Requested

Approved

Denied

Processed

Reversed

Refund history shall be preserved.

---

# 10. Taxes

The Commerce System shall support:

Regional Tax Rules

VAT

GST

Sales Tax

Tax Exempt Organizations

Government Procurement

Tax handling is implementation-defined and jurisdiction-specific.

---

# 11. Payment Providers

Commerce shall support multiple payment providers.

Examples include:

Credit Card Processors

Bank Transfer

Digital Wallets

Institution Billing

Enterprise Invoicing

Government Procurement Systems

Payment providers are modular.

---

# 12. Subscriptions

Subscriptions may provide access to:

Publishers

Collections

Engineering Domains

Training Libraries

Support Services

Enterprise Resources

Subscription renewal policies are publisher-defined.

---

# 13. Promotions

Publishers may create:

Discount Codes

Seasonal Sales

Educational Discounts

Bundle Discounts

Launch Promotions

Partner Promotions

Promotion history is retained.

---

# 14. Bundles

Commercial bundles may include:

Multiple Packages

Collections

Training

Support

Documentation

Certification

Bundles remain independent commercial entities.

---

# 15. Financial Reporting

The Exchange shall provide reporting for:

Publishers

Organizations

Institutions

Enterprise Customers

Government Customers

Reports may include exports for accounting systems.

---

# 16. Audit

Commerce audit records include:

Purchase

Refund

Subscription Renewal

Revenue Distribution

Payment Failure

Promotion Applied

Voucher Redeemed

Invoice Generated

Financial audit records are immutable.

---

# 17. Future Extensions

Future specifications may introduce:

Engineering grants

Crowdfunding

Community sponsorship

Marketplace advertising

Usage royalties

Creator subscriptions

Engineering bounties

Marketplace escrow

without altering the commerce architecture.

---

# 18. Conformance

An implementation claiming compliance with EXC-010 shall:

- Separate commerce from licensing.
- Preserve financial audit history.
- Support configurable pricing models.
- Support multiple payment providers.
- Support publisher-defined commercial offerings.
- Support transparent revenue reporting.
