# 1. Purpose

This document defines the typography system for the OEP Instruments platform.

Typography communicates engineering information.

It shall maximize readability, minimize ambiguity, and establish a consistent visual identity across every OEP Instrument.

---

# 2. Philosophy

Professional engineering instruments are designed to be read instantly.

Typography shall never draw attention to itself.

It shall communicate information with precision.

The engineer should never struggle to distinguish one character from another.

---

# 3. Design Objectives

Typography shall be:

Highly Readable

Professional

Timeless

Consistent

Accessible

Deterministic

Platform Independent

---

# 4. Typeface Categories

The platform shall define:

Display Typeface

Interface Typeface

Engineering Typeface

Monospaced Typeface

Documentation Typeface

Future typefaces

Each category has a distinct purpose.

---

# 5. Display Typeface

Display typography presents engineering measurements.

Requirements:

Large

Monospaced

Stable width

High contrast

Excellent readability

No decorative features

The display typeface shall become recognizable as part of the OEP identity.

---

# 6. User Interface Typeface

Used for:

Menus

Dialogs

Buttons

Settings

Navigation

Help

Characteristics:

Neutral

Professional

Readable

Compact

Consistent

---

# 7. Engineering Typeface

Used for:

Units

Connector names

Pin numbers

Wire labels

Engineering identifiers

Signal names

The engineering typeface shall prioritize character distinction.

---

# 8. Monospaced Typeface

Used for:

Measurements

Tables

Logs

Protocol messages

Terminal output

Diagnostics

Fixed-width alignment shall never shift during updates.

---

# 9. Character Distinction

Characters shall remain unmistakable.

Examples:

0 vs O

1 vs I vs l

5 vs S

8 vs B

2 vs Z

Engineering environments shall never permit ambiguous characters.

---

# 10. Numeric Presentation

Measurements shall:

Align vertically

Maintain fixed width

Avoid horizontal shifting

Support engineering notation

Support negative values

Support scientific notation

Numbers shall remain visually stable during updates.

---

Consistent line spacing

Consistent paragraph spacing

Resize during updates

Shift measurements

Flicker

Animate unnecessarily

Engineering stability is more important than visual effects.

Typography shall support:

Engineering symbols

Imperial

# 18. Accessibility

---

Future instruments may introduce specialized typography where required.

Specialized typography shall remain compatible with the overall OEP design language.
